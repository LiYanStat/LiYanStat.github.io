Biography

I am an assistant professor in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at Auburn University. My research centers on developing high-dimensional statistical methods for large-scale, complex data, with applications in environmental and climate science, microbiome research, and population health.

Before joining Auburn in August 2023, I was a postdoctoral fellow in the Department of Biostatistics at the University of Michigan (2021–2023), working with Dr. Gen Li. I received my PhD in Statistics from the University of Connecticut in 2021, advised by Dr. Kun Chen and Dr. Jun Yan, and my BS in Statistics from Renmin University of China in 2016.

My work connects statistical methodology with scientific applications and open-source software. See my research directions, R packages, and Google Scholar profile.

Software

I develop open-source R packages for statistical methodology and scientific applications.

  • dacc — Detection and attribution analysis of climate change.
  • tls — Total least squares for measurement error models.
  • paaPack — Principal amalgamation analysis for microbiome data.
  • touch — Tools for healthcare utilization and cost data.
  • fmerPack — Heterogeneity pursuit via finite mixture effects regression.
